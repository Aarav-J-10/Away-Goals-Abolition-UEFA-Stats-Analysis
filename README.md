# Away-Goals Abolition: UEFA Two-Legged Knockout Ties Before and After 2021

Data and code for the paper **"Rule Change and Competitive Dynamics: A Statistical Analysis of UEFA Two-Legged Knockout
Ties Before and After Away-Goals Abolition"** (A. Jalan, 2026).

The repository contains a **verified, tie-level dataset of all 533 two-legged knockout ties** of the UEFA Champions League
and UEFA Europa League in 2012/13–2019/20 (away-goals rule active) and 2021/22–2025/26 (rule abolished), plus the full
pipeline that verifies, cleans and analyses it. Every number, table and figure in the paper is regenerated by one command.

## Key findings

| Question | Answer (primary like-for-like sample, R16–SF: 210 pre vs 139 post ties) |
|---|---|
| Did scoring change? | No. Second-leg goals (90 min) 2.97 vs 3.02; statistically **equivalent** within ±0.5 goals (TOST p = 0.012). |
| Do more ties end level after 180 min? | No. 18.6% vs 15.1% (p = 0.40). |
| Why are there more extra-time periods and shoot-outs? | **Mechanically.** Re-scoring each tie under the other era's rule: the rule switch alone adds +8.4 pp extra time. In the full 533-tie sample the whole rise (+9.2 pp extra time, +5.0 pp shoot-outs) is mechanical (+8.3 pp and +5.1 pp). |
| Did abolition change who advanced? | Yes, in **11 of 195** post-reform ties (e.g. Man City v Real Madrid 2023/24, Atlético v Inter 2023/24). |
| Did comebacks change? | Overall rate unchanged, but comebacks from 2+ goal deficits fell from 17.2% to 3.9% while 1-goal comebacks rose (era × deficit interaction OR 0.08, Holm-adjusted p = 0.033; robust in 6 specifications). |

## Reproduce

```bash
python -m pip install -r requirements.txt
python run_all.py
```

Runtime is about two minutes. Bootstrap and permutation procedures use a fixed seed (`src/common.py`), so repeated runs
give byte-identical `results/results.json`. Tested with Python 3.13.

## Data provenance and verification

* **Raw data**: leg scores, extra-time and penalty indicators, winners and notes for each tie, compiled by the author.
* **Independent checks**: openfootball (all UCL seasons; UEL 2021/22–2024/25) and Wikipedia knockout-phase pages
  (UEL 2012/13–2019/20, 2025/26). **1,066 / 1,066 leg scores match**; no tie is missing or duplicated; every recorded winner
  agrees with the tie-breaking rule of its era. Details: `docs/VERIFICATION_REPORT.md`.
* **Scope**: finals (single match), the 2020/21 season (played without spectators), 2019/20 ties completed as single matches
  in August 2020, and the cancelled RB Leipzig v Spartak Moscow tie (2021/22) are not included. 2019/20 is flagged.
* **Samples**: `sample_primary` (R16, QF, SF; same rounds in both eras) is the main analysis sample; `sample_extended` adds the
  UEL Round of 32 (pre) and knockout play-offs (post).

## Methods in brief

Welch, Mann–Whitney, permutation and TOST equivalence tests; two-proportion z and Fisher exact tests with Wilson/Newcombe
intervals; Poisson and logistic regression (robust SEs; Firth for rare outcomes); Holm correction across seven primary
hypotheses; a counterfactual decomposition that re-scores every tie under the other era's tie-break rule (bootstrap CIs);
nine robustness specifications; two placebo splits; power analysis.
